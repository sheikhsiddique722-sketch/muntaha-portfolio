Overview

Project Overview

FutureSelf AI Mirror is an introspective, AI-powered experience designed to help individuals visualise the most aspirational version of themselves. By combining carefully engineered reflective prompts with generative imagery and narrative storytelling, the project creates a deeply personal and emotionally resonant encounter with possibility.

The concept emerged from a desire to make AI feel intimate rather than transactional. Rather than producing generic outputs, every element of the experience is shaped by the user's own words — their values, their goals, their vision of a life well-lived. The AI becomes a mirror, not a machine.

The project was developed as both a creative experiment and a demonstration of advanced prompt engineering technique — specifically, how layered context-setting and structured input can coax language models and image generators toward highly personalised, emotionally intelligent outputs.

Experience

The Journey

01
Reflection Prompts
The user answers a curated set of introspective questions about values, aspirations, and self-perception.
02
AI Synthesis
Responses are processed through a structured prompt pipeline to extract themes, tone, and a personalised narrative arc.
03
Visual Generation
Midjourney and DALL·E prompts are crafted dynamically from the synthesis to produce bespoke future-self imagery.
04
Narrative Reveal
A short narrative letter — written by the AI as the user's future self — is presented alongside the imagery.

Highlights

Key Features

  • Context-chained prompt pipeline that preserves user intent across multiple AI model calls
  • Dynamic Midjourney prompt construction based on user-reported traits, aesthetic preferences, and aspirational keywords
  • Dual-output format: a visual portrait paired with a personalised narrative letter written in the user's voice
  • Reflective question set designed with psychological depth — balancing concrete goals with emotional resonance
  • Tone calibration layer that adjusts the narrative register from aspirational to grounded based on user responses
  • Iterative refinement loop — users can adjust emphasis and regenerate specific elements without starting over
  • Presentation layer designed to feel like an intimate reveal rather than a chatbot interaction
  • Showcases advanced prompt engineering: role assignment, persona seeding, negative prompting, and style anchoring
