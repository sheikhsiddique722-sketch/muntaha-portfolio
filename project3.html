Overview

Project Overview

This project is a full-spectrum content strategy for producing motivational video content at scale — without sacrificing quality, authenticity, or emotional depth. Using generative AI throughout the production pipeline, it demonstrates how a solo creator or small team can consistently produce content that competes with established channels.

The strategy addresses every layer of the content lifecycle: ideation and niche positioning, scriptwriting frameworks, visual and audio direction, thumbnail and title optimisation, and multi-platform distribution tailored to algorithmic behaviour on YouTube, Instagram Reels, and TikTok.

At its core, this is a demonstration of AI as a creative collaborator rather than a replacement — the human brings the vision, the audience understanding, and the emotional intelligence; the AI handles volume, variation, and optimisation at every stage.

Strategic Pillars

Platform-specific title, description, tag, and thumbnail strategies calibrated to YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram algorithms.

Highlights

Key Features

  • Repeatable AI-assisted scriptwriting framework — a fill-in-the-structure system that maintains emotional authenticity at speed
  • Hook bank: 30+ AI-generated opening hooks categorised by psychological trigger (curiosity, contrast, challenge, identity)
  • Visual brief templates for briefing AI image and video tools to produce on-brand, cinematic output every time
  • Thumbnail formula combining AI-generated imagery with proven text-overlay compositions for high click-through rates
  • Platform-specific adaptation guides — same core content restructured for long-form, short-form, and vertical formats
  • SEO and metadata strategy: title structures, keyword integration, and description templates optimised per platform
  • Content calendar framework with topic clustering and seasonal relevance mapping for consistent publishing
  • AI voiceover prototyping workflow using ElevenLabs for rapid iteration before final recording
  • Performance review checklist — 10 signals to track per video to continuously refine the strategy
